Frequently Asked Questions

After uploading, each page appears as a numbered thumbnail. Click any page to mark it for removal, then click Remove Selected Pages.

Can I remove multiple pages at once?

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

Remove Pages from a PDF Online

This tool lets you delete unwanted pages from a PDF file directly in your browser, without needing Adobe Acrobat or any desktop software. Upload your PDF, select the pages you want to remove, and download a clean version with just those pages gone.

When You Need to Remove PDF Pages

  • Removing a blank scanned page that got included accidentally during scanning.
  • Trimming a cover page or table of contents before sharing a document section.
  • Deleting a signature page or personal information page before forwarding a document.
  • Cutting down a large document to just the relevant sections for a specific recipient.

How This Tool Works

After uploading, the tool renders a numbered list of every page in your PDF. Click any page to mark it for removal — marked pages are visually highlighted. When you click Remove Selected Pages, the tool uses the pdf-lib library to build a new PDF document containing only the pages you didn't mark, preserving the original page order and content for everything that remains.

Privacy and Local Processing

All page removal happens locally in your browser using pdf-lib — your PDF is never uploaded to a server, which keeps sensitive or confidential documents completely private.
