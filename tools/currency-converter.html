Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need to enter my own exchange rate?

This tool works entirely offline in your browser without connecting to a live financial data service, so you enter the current rate yourself from a source you trust, and always know exactly which rate was used in the calculation.

Where can I find the current exchange rate?

Your bank, a financial news website, or a currency data provider will show current rates. Rates fluctuate constantly, so check a live source close to when you need the conversion.

Can I convert any currency pair?

Yes, enter any two currency codes and the exchange rate between them — the tool works for any currency pair, not just a fixed list.

Does this tool store my conversion history?

Recent conversions are kept only in your browser's memory for the current session and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Currency Converter With Custom Exchange Rate

This currency converter lets you convert between any two currencies using an exchange rate you provide, rather than relying on a live data feed from a third-party financial API. This design makes the tool fast, reliable, and completely private — there's no dependency on an external service that could be rate-limited, go down, or require an API key.

Why a Manual-Rate Converter?

Live currency APIs are useful, but they come with trade-offs: they require constant internet access to a third-party service, are subject to rate limits or downtime, and often require an API key that isn't practical to expose in a purely client-side tool. By instead letting you enter the current exchange rate directly — which you can look up from your bank, a financial news site, or any currency data provider you trust — this tool stays fast, works reliably, and gives you full transparency over exactly which rate is being used in the calculation, rather than a rate that updates silently behind the scenes.

How to Use This Converter

  1. Look up the current exchange rate between your two currencies from a reliable source (your bank, XE, Google Finance, or similar).
  2. Enter the currency codes (like USD, EUR, INR, GBP) for reference.
  3. Enter the exchange rate as "how many units of the target currency equal 1 unit of the source currency."
  4. Enter the amount you want to convert and click Convert.

Understanding Exchange Rate Direction

Exchange rates are always directional — the rate for USD to INR is different from (and the mathematical inverse of) the rate for INR to USD. This tool expects the rate as "1 unit of the From currency equals X units of the To currency," which matches how most rate lookups are typically displayed. The Swap button lets you quickly reverse the direction if you need to convert the other way, automatically inverting the rate for you.

Privacy and Local Processing

All conversion math happens directly in your browser — no amounts, currency codes, or rates you enter are sent to any server.
