Frequently Asked Questions

Does this show my real IP address or location?

No, this tool only shows information your browser voluntarily reports via JavaScript, such as browser type, OS, screen size, timezone, and language. It does not perform IP-based geolocation.

Why does my timezone show but not my city?

Your browser reports its configured timezone (like Asia/Kolkata) directly, but does not report your city or precise location unless you separately grant location permission.

Is this information sent anywhere?

No, everything is read and displayed entirely within your browser — nothing is transmitted to a server.

What Your Browser Reports About Itself

This tool displays a range of information that web browsers make available to JavaScript running on any page — details like your browser name and version, operating system, screen resolution, timezone, and language preference. It's important to understand what this actually is: browser-self-reported metadata, not IP-based geolocation or any form of precise identification.

What This Tool Does NOT Do

This tool does not look up your IP address, does not determine your city or physical location, and does not access any server-side geolocation database. Real IP geolocation requires server-side infrastructure that looks up an IP address against a database of network ranges — something this purely browser-based tool doesn't do. Any location-sounding information shown here (like your timezone) comes directly from your device's own settings, not from analyzing your network connection.

What Information Is Actually Shown

  • Browser and version — parsed from the browser's user agent string.
  • Operating system — also derived from the user agent string.
  • Screen resolution — your display's pixel dimensions, from the browser's screen API.
  • Viewport size — the current browser window's visible content area.
  • Timezone — your device's configured timezone setting.
  • Language — your browser's configured language preference.
  • Cookies enabled — whether your browser currently accepts cookies.
  • Online status — whether your browser currently detects an internet connection.

Why This Information Matters

Understanding what a website can learn about your browser just from standard JavaScript APIs — without any special tracking or permissions — helps demystify a common source of confusion about "fingerprinting" and privacy. This same information (browser, OS, screen size, timezone, language) is available to essentially any website you visit, forming part of what's sometimes called a browser fingerprint when combined across multiple signals.

Privacy and Local Processing

All information shown here is read directly from your browser's JavaScript APIs and displayed locally — nothing is transmitted to any server by this tool.
