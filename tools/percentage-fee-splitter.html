Frequently Asked Questions

What is a percentage plus fixed fee structure?

Many payment processors and marketplaces charge a percentage of the transaction plus a small fixed amount, such as 2.9% + $0.30, a common structure among payment gateways worldwide.

Can I use this for any currency?

Yes, this calculator works with plain numbers and applies to any currency — enter your amount and fee structure regardless of currency.

Does this account for taxes?

No, this tool calculates only the platform or processing fee deduction. Tax obligations vary by jurisdiction and are not calculated here.

Calculate Your Exact Net Payout After Fees

This tool calculates exactly how much of a transaction amount you'll actually receive after a platform, payment processor, or marketplace deducts its fee — which is typically structured as a percentage of the transaction plus a small fixed amount per transaction.

How Percentage + Fixed Fees Work

Most payment processors and online marketplaces don't charge just a flat percentage — they combine a percentage rate with a small fixed fee per transaction. For example, a processor charging "2.9% + $0.30" on a $100 sale takes $2.90 (2.9% of $100) plus an additional $0.30 flat fee, for a total deduction of $3.20, leaving a net payout of $96.80. This combined structure means the effective fee percentage is actually higher on smaller transactions (where the fixed fee makes up a larger share) than on larger ones.

Where This Fee Structure Appears

  • Payment gateways — services processing card payments typically use this percentage-plus-fixed model.
  • Online marketplaces — platforms selling goods or services often deduct a percentage-based commission, sometimes with an additional listing or transaction fee.
  • Freelance and gig platforms — many charge a percentage fee on payments between clients and freelancers.
  • Crowdfunding platforms — typically deduct a percentage plus payment processing fees from funds raised.

Why Calculate This in Advance

Understanding your exact net payout before setting a price or accepting a payment helps you price products and services accurately, forecast actual take-home revenue, and compare different platforms' fee structures on equal footing. A platform with a lower percentage but higher fixed fee might actually cost you more on small transactions than a platform with a higher percentage but no fixed fee — this calculator lets you check the real numbers for your specific transaction size.
