Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert annual salary to hourly rate?

Divide annual salary by total hours worked per year — typically hours per week × weeks per year (commonly 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours).

Does this account for taxes?

No, this converts between gross pay periods only. Actual take-home pay after taxes and deductions will be lower and varies by jurisdiction.

What if I don't work 52 weeks a year?

Adjust the "Weeks Worked per Year" field to reflect actual weeks worked, such as 50 weeks if you take 2 weeks of unpaid leave.

Can I use this for part-time work?

Yes, adjust "Hours per Week" to your actual part-time schedule for accurate conversions.

How the Conversion Works

All conversions are built from a base calculation: total hours worked per year, found by multiplying hours per week by weeks worked per year. A standard full-time schedule of 40 hours per week for 52 weeks yields 2,080 hours per year — the baseline most salary-to-hourly comparisons use. From there, annual salary divided by annual hours gives the hourly rate; that hourly rate multiplied by hours per day, week, or month gives each other period's equivalent pay.

Why Convert Between Pay Periods

  • Comparing job offers — one offer quoted as an annual salary and another as an hourly rate can be hard to compare directly without conversion.
  • Freelance and contract pricing — professionals moving from salaried to hourly (or vice versa) work need an accurate baseline conversion.
  • Budgeting — breaking an annual salary into monthly or weekly figures makes budgeting more concrete.
  • Overtime and part-time calculations — understanding your true hourly rate helps evaluate overtime pay or part-time schedule changes.

A Note on Gross vs. Net Pay

This calculator works with gross pay — the amount before taxes, insurance, retirement contributions, and other deductions. Actual take-home (net) pay will be lower and depends heavily on your specific tax situation, benefits elections, and local regulations, which vary too widely to calculate generically.
